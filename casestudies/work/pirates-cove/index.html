01 — The Brief

Not just a waterpark.
An adventure.

Most local waterparks look like clip art and Groupon deals. Pirates’ Cove needed a brand strong enough to compete with DFW’s major family destinations — starting day one of their Grand Opening season.

piratescovefunzone.com
02 — Identity

Bold, sun-soaked,
unmistakably pirate.

The brand leans into the adventure — deep pirate red, weathered sand, and dark mahogany. Ferryman brings swashbuckling personality to every headline. Proxima Nova keeps the experience legible and trustworthy for parents making quick decisions.
