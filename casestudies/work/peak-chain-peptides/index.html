The Brief

Build a research compound platform credible enough for scientists, clear enough for newcomers.

Most peptide sites read like supplement brands in a lab coat. Peakchain needed to earn trust with researchers — real documentation standards, real literature citations, and a visual language that says “institution,” not “influencer.”

02 — Identity

Precise.
Institutional.
Trusted.

The identity anchors on a deep navy-to-cyan spectrum — clinical without being cold, authoritative without being inaccessible. IBM Plex Serif light for editorial weight, IBM Plex Mono for data labels and eyebrows, IBM Plex Sans for body. Three faces of one family. One standard of precision.

03 — Research-First UX

Education before the catalog. Always.

Every compound page leads with the research library — published mechanisms of action, peer-reviewed citations, pathway data. The catalog comes second. This isn't friction. It's the standard.

04 — The Work

Three pillars.
One platform.

Every design decision maps to a research principle: hierarchy that earns trust before asking for a sale, typography that signals precision, documentation that is never buried.

Platform Hero

A research-first hero section that leads with credibility signals — purity floors, lot verification, and documentation links before any product pitch.

Compound Catalog

Clean compound cards with HPLC data, CAS numbers, and purity specs surfaced at a glance. Every entry links to a full research dossier.

Research Library

A dedicated hub for peer-reviewed literature, mechanism-of-action breakdowns, and pathway maps — the educational backbone of the platform.

05 — Documentation Standard
“Documentation accuracy is not a courtesy.
It is a research requirement.”
— Peak Chain Peptides · Core Platform Principle
06 — The Long Scroll

One page.
One standard.

Trust → Research → Catalog → Compliance. A single narrative that earns credibility before it asks for anything. The design respects a researcher's time.
