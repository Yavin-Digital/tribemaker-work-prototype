The Brief

Make a careful inspection feel as direct as the phone call.

The live site at kisseeinspections.com is the current Next.js build. Navy, gold, and the lion-and-skyline mark carry a long menu of inspection types without burying the estimate.

02 — Identity

Navy for the work. Gold for the call.

Poppins carries the headlines. Open Sans carries the body. The mark is a lion against a DFW skyline and a house — gold field, blue linework — and it sits in the header of every page on the live site.
